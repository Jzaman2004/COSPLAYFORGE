### Where to Get Real Assets

**Option 1: DIY (Cheapest)**
- **YouTube Demo**: CapCut (free) - screen record + edit
- **Try-On Images**: Nano Banana (nanobananax.com) or GIMP
- **Voices**: ElevenLabs free tier (10k chars/month)
- **NFT**: Canva (free template)
- **Figma**: Figma.com (free account)
- **Total**: ~35 mins, completely free ✨

**Option 2: Commission (Faster)**
- Fiverr: Video editing ($20-30)
- Fiverr: Graphic design ($15-25)
- ElevenLabs paid tier ($11/month)
- **Total**: $50-70, 2-3 days

**Option 3: Hybrid (Recommended)**
- DIY the easy stuff (voices, NFT, Figma - 20 mins)
- Commission the video (Fiverr - 1 day, $25)
- Generate overlays (Nano Banana free - 10 mins)
- **Total**: $25, ~1 day

```bash

# Images

# Audio

```

No code changes needed. SVG placeholders auto-swap when real files appear.
